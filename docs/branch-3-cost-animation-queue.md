## Wraithfire Bolt — reference notes
Reference: `uploads/piccolo gif.gif` (25 frames, ~2.5 s; contact sheet `qa/ref/piccolo-sheet.png`). Motion reference only — Branch keeps his own look.
Beats taken from it, adapted to Branch:
1. Charge (~0.4 s): wide braced stance, glove pulled to chest, small embers gather on the glove.
2. Thrust: punching glove locks straight out toward the target; a bright fire orb flickers at the knuckles.
3. Fire: a straight flame core with a spinning fire spiral wrapped around it shoots from the glove, capped by a bright fireball head, and grows to reach the target.
4. Impact + quick fade; arm recovers. Shortened from the reference's 2.5 s to ~1.2-1.5 s to keep combat moving.
Split: Branch's body (charge + thrust pose) and the beam as a separate effect, so the beam can stretch to whichever enemy is targeted.
