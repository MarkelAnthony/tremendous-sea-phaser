# The Tremendous Sea — visual production direction

Authoritative source: docs/handoff/ART_DIRECTION.md, ASSET_LIST.md, and GAME_OVERVIEW.md, preserved verbatim from Tremendous_Sea_PixelLab_Handoff.zip. These supersede the older prototype brief. This is a staged art rollout, not authorization to replace game mechanics.

Use the supplied master style: black outer outlines, darker colored inner lines, dithered shading, top-right lighting, roughly 32 shared colors; backgrounds muted beneath readable characters. The handoff's 640×360 base-resolution migration is a separate future task, not an automatic change to the current 1000×700 runtime.

The scope also includes crew portraits, captain portraits, Gear illustrations, status/intent icons, logo, map node icons, and boss arenas. Follow the exact supplied designs and phased checklist rather than inventing substitutes. Keep existing approved character identities unless the creator approves a redesign.

## Shared identity
Pirate fantasy where weaponized candy masks increasingly unsettling ocean mysteries. Bold readable silhouettes, consistent pixel scale, carefully composed battle stages. Candy is a material applied to anatomy and equipment, not a substitute for recognizable creatures. Preserve approved captain identities and existing clips.

## Scope beyond ships
- Captains: preserve approved likenesses; consistent portraits, idle/attack/hurt/death coverage where needed.
- Enemies: bosses, elites, commons in handoff order; readable identities instead of generic placeholder bodies.
- Cards: distinct thematic illustrations with unobstructed titles, costs, and rules.
- Effects: captain-specific attacks, impacts, status effects, and clear turn feedback.
- Interface: coherent nautical menus, selection, map, rewards, shops, and battle HUD; readable and accessible.
- Ships: individual on-deck arenas aligned with captain identity.
- Act environments: deferred until ship identities are approved; Ocean of Tears (worn/lightly cursed), Candied Ocean (excessive glossy sweetness), Eternal Dark (sweet surface concealing cosmic wrongness).

## First visual milestone: Kindleroot battle deck
Generate one representative arena and integrate it for Branch. Combat occurs ON the timber deck, never in front of a distant full ship. Include a giant living tree mast, leafy sails, gold tree-and-root railing carvings, colorful trees with visible trunks and forked branches, ember-fissured boards, potted saplings. Keep the fight lane open and text/UI separate. Review this stage before expanding the asset family.

## Review and implementation
One deliberate visual milestone at a time. Follow handoff details for each asset, preserve existing gameplay and approved art, verify the actual battle composition in-browser. Further character/object generation requires an approved generation plan. Do not assume approval of every asset in the entire handoff from approval of this first stage.
